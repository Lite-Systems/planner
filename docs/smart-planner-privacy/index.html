Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at Smart Planner ("Company", "we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy notice, or our practices with regard to your personal information, please contact us.

When you use our mobile application (the "App"), we appreciate that you are trusting us with your personal information. We take your privacy very seriously. In this privacy notice, we seek to explain to you in the clearest way possible what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1What Information Do We Collect?

Personal information you disclose to us

You may use the App without an account. In guest mode, projects, tasks, finances, notes, files, and preferences are stored locally. If you choose to create an account with email and password, Firebase Authentication processes those credentials and returns an account identifier and authentication session. Your planner records and selected preferences are synchronized through Cloud Firestore so you can use them on other devices. Images and attachments remain local to each device. Importing existing guest data into an account is optional.

Information collected through third-party services

The App uses these services for the features you choose:

  • Firebase Authentication: Creates and authenticates optional email/password accounts, sends password-reset emails, and manages authentication tokens. The App does not persist your password.
  • Cloud Firestore: Hosts private planner records and synchronization versions. Database security rules restrict each user to their own records. Hosting administrators may have operational access; this service is not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Speech To Text: Voice recognition data is processed by your device's native speech recognition services (e.g., Google Speech Services on Android, Apple Speech on iOS) according to their respective privacy policies.

2How Do We Use Your Information?

Guest data stays on your device unless you import, export, or share it. Signed-in account data is processed to store your records and synchronize changes across your devices.

We use account and synchronization information to:

  • Authenticate your account and synchronize the planner records and files you choose to save.
  • Resolve synchronization conflicts, maintain service reliability, and complete requested account deletion.

3Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any of your information with third parties for their promotional purposes.

4How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

Local records and files remain until you remove the App’s data. Signing out preserves unsynced changes privately on that device for the same account. Importing guest data creates a local backup; guest data and exported backups remain independent copies. Cloud planner records and synchronization versions remain until account deletion. The free plan does not upload images or attachments. Use Settings → Account & Sync → Delete account and cloud data to delete your hosted account and cloud planner records. A minimal deletion marker is retained to prevent another device’s unexpired session from recreating deleted data. If deletion is interrupted, retry it to finish cleanup. Uninstalling alone does not delete cloud data. Delete downloaded copies and backups separately on other devices; an offline device cannot be remotely erased.

5How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

Authentication sessions are stored using the operating system’s secure credential storage. Cloud connections use HTTPS and hosted records are restricted to their account. The local SQLite database and cached files are protected by your operating system’s app storage; they are not additionally encrypted by this feature. Optional biometric app lock restricts access through the app. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

6Do We Make Updates To This Notice?

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last updated" date, and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible. We encourage you to review this privacy notice frequently to be informed of how we are protecting your information.
